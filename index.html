        About the Hub

        Riverside Council runs the Hub with local NHS teams, charities and volunteers. It opened in 2026 so residents can find support close to home, in one building and online.

        Cost
        Almost everything is free. A few classes cost £2.
        Referral
        Not needed for most services. Just drop in or book.
        Opening times
        Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 4pm
        Access
        Step-free entrance, hearing loop, free parking and interpreters on request

    Common questions

    Do I need a referral?

    No. Most services are drop-in or self-referral. A few, like one-to-one counselling, start with a short chat to make sure it's the right fit.

    Is it free?

    Almost everything is free. A few classes cost £2 a session, and we never want cost to stop anyone taking part, so just ask.

    Can I bring someone with me?

    Yes. You're welcome to bring a friend, family member or carer.

    I don't use the internet. How can I book?

    Call us on 0300 123 4567 or come to reception and we'll book for you.

    Do you offer support in other languages?

    Yes. We can arrange interpreters, including British Sign Language, free of charge. Just let us know when you book.
